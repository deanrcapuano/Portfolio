About Me

Previous Design Representative | Present Day Dev


Hello, I am Dean Capuano! Welcome to my home-made website!
I am a digital designer and programmer. I have over five years of experience in those fields as well as management and customer service.

I attended Arizona State University where I graduated with a 4.1 GPA from the Herberger School of Arts acquiring a Bachelors of Science degree in Digital Cultures and Media Processing.

During that time I worked at the fabrication lab planning production and scheduling construction of student projects. Many of which taught me how to integrate digital and physical design into everyday objects and media.

After graduation I was hired by Grads Photography as a designer for their growing yearbook department. While working for them I also was tasked with managing support (sales and assistance), establishing marketing, working with over two dozen schools as their main contact, and creating programs that aided in providing a more efficient workflow.

I have developed the skills and knowledge of 3D and 2D design, programming, project management, scheduling, technical support, handling of finance and sales, as well as many other tools necessary for project development.

Below are a few of the programs I am proficient in and use on a daily basis. Even further below is a list of works I have made in the past that I am able to show you.

Thank you so much for your time checking out my website and I hope you have a wonderful day!

Pixar's Up Themed Cover

3D Blender Modeled Building/Prototype
3D Blender Modeled Balloons
Initial Three Cover Designs
Finalized Cover Design

The most complex cover I made as a yearbook representative. This request was for a book in the style of Pixar's “Up”. I began by creating a basic render in Blender 3D of a house with balloons attached. Once that concept was approved, I began working on a more detailed version. Creating the building, I structured it based on images provided to me of the school.
Once complete and rendered, I next created the balloon models. Duplicating them, I added a shader that randomly assigned a color of a specified range and value. I compiled it together in Clip Studio Paint, coloring the building and background. Finishing it up by aligning it in Illustrator and finalizing the background and clouds in Photoshop.
Three variants were made. From their selection, we moved forward with the completed design.
Alongside my other duties, I completed this design over three weeks, and it became one of my most memorable projects!

Data Converter Program

Early Data Converter Code Ref
Data Converter Design

This program I made and designed under a tight schedual helped us filter and process duplicate image files. These files were causing issues for students who had multiple photos taken throughout the year. The program quickly filtered the photos via name and class. It prioritized our requested fall photos opting to ignore the duplicates taken in spring (unless specifically requested). This program filtered the photos in seconds, saving us hours/days of work that year.
